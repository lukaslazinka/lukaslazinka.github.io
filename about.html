Profile

Lukas Lazinka is a Lithuanian composer based in Vilnius. His work explores contemporary musical language, structure, colour and expressive contrast.

His compositional practice includes concert music and independent creative projects, with an interest in combining rigorous musical systems with an individual sonic identity.
