Biography

About

Musical education, performance background and compositional profile.

Lukas Lazinka was born in 2002 in Ignalina, Lithuania. His musical journey began in 2009 when he enrolled in Ignalina Česlovas Kudaba primary school, now known as Ignalina Česlovas Kudaba gymnasium, and at the same time started his studies at Ignalina music school, accordion class, which was later reorganised into the Ignalina Mikas Petrauskas music school, class XLIX. While studying at the music school, Lukas took part in various concerts together with the music school's ensembles both in Ignalina district and abroad.

In 2017 Lukas started his studies at the Vilnius Juozas Tallat-Kelpša conservatory, accordion specialty, class XVII. At the conservatory he continued his development in accordion, actively participating in concerts, competitions and festivals in Lithuania and abroad. His performances, both solo and in ensembles, have been recognised and awarded prizes.

In the twelfth grade of the Conservatory, Lukas started studying composition with Prof. Ričardas Kabelis, which became an important aspect of his musical activity, opening a great interest in creativity and compositional techniques. In 2021, Lukas enrolled at the Lithuanian Academy of Music and Theatre, Department of Composition, continuing his studies with Prof. Ričardas Kabelis. His journey in the world of music is characterised by continuous development and creativity. Ignalina Young Talent of the Year 2016.

Artistic profile

Style of music

It tries to convey the sense that the music is varied, sensual, otherworldly, and at the same time created according to compositional systems. The music is full of classical harmony chords intertwined with pop-style musical motifs.

Currently working on electronic dance music. The music is uploaded to various music portals such as YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music, etc. The music is protected by copyright associations (AGATA, LATGA).
